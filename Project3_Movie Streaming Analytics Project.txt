
--Movie Streaming Analytics Project  

Business Scenario

A streaming platform (like Netflix) wants to analyze:

QUESTIONS---

  Total Monthly Revenue (Active Users Only)
  Revenue by Plan
  Most Watched Movies
  Total Watch Time per User
  User Ranking by Watch Time (Window Function)
  Monthly New Users
  Top Genre by Watch Time

   STEP 1: Create Database
   STEP 2: Create Tables
 
Users-- UserID ,FullName ,City ,JoinDate 
 
SubscriptionPlans--  PlanID, PlanName, MonthlyFee  
 

Subscriptions-- SubscriptionID, UserID, PlanID, StartDate,
                Status VARCHAR(20), -- Active / Cancelled,
    FOREIGN KEY (UserID) REFERENCES Users(UserID),
    FOREIGN KEY (PlanID) REFERENCES SubscriptionPlans(PlanID)
 

 Movies-- MovieID, Title, Genre 

WatchHistory-- WatchID, UserID ,MovieID, WatchDate, WatchTimeMinutes, 
    FOREIGN KEY (UserID) REFERENCES Users(UserID),
    FOREIGN KEY (MovieID) REFERENCES Movies(MovieID)
